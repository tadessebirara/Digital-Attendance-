The Challenge

Managing a workforce the old way is costly, error-prone, and insecure

📝

Paper Registers & Spreadsheets

Manual records are lost, altered, or simply wrong. HR spends 15–20% of their time just managing attendance paperwork.

👥

Buddy Punching

Employees clock in for absent colleagues. Without verification, payroll is paying for people who aren't there.

🔍

No Real-Time Visibility

Managers learn about absences and late arrivals at the end of the day — when it's too late to act.

🗂️

Fragmented HR Processes

Leave requests go through email, schedule changes through WhatsApp, payroll through separate software. Nothing talks to anything.

🔒

No Audit Trail

When a dispute arises, there's no reliable record of who changed what, when, and from where. Legal and compliance risk escalates.

📡

Poor Connectivity Blocks Work

Field employees in low-signal areas can't record attendance. Data gaps create retroactive manual corrections that nobody trusts.

What Makes Us Different

Unique features you won't find in typical systems

🔐

Device-Locked Attendance

Every employee's attendance is cryptographically signed by their registered phone. Even if someone knows your password, they can't clock in for you from a different device. Buddy punching becomes technically impossible.

⚖️

Smart Conflict Resolution

When multiple sources update the same record, a database engine enforces a priority order: HR corrections always win. An offline sync from 3 hours later can never overwrite an HR manual entry. The most authoritative record always wins.

📶

True Offline-First

This is not just "works sometimes offline." Every action — clock-in, leave request, message — is queued locally with a unique identifier and automatically delivered once you reconnect, in the exact right order, with zero duplicates.

🇪🇹

Ethiopian Calendar Integration

Built-in Ethiopian calendar utility and a complete Ethiopian public holiday database. Schedule calculations, leave approvals, and attendance status all respect local business context — including bilingual English/Amharic holiday names.

♻️

Rotating QR Tokens

QR codes for attendance aren't static images. They're cryptographically signed, expire after a set time, and can only be used once. The secret behind them rotates automatically, making pre-photographed QR codes worthless.

🛡️

Stolen Token Protection

If anyone tries to reuse a stolen login session, the system detects it instantly and terminates every active session for that user across all devices — automatically, in real time.

Enterprise-Grade Security

Built secure from the ground up — not bolted on

🔑

Multi-Layer Login

Password + email OTP + device verification. Three layers before access is granted. Account locks after 5 failed attempts.

📱

One Device Per Employee

Each employee's account is bound to one approved phone. New devices require HR/Admin approval before they work.

🔄

Automatic Session Rotation

Login tokens automatically refresh and the old one is destroyed. No credentials stay valid forever.

📝

Everything is Logged

Every login, attendance record, leave approval, role change, and file upload — permanently logged with full context. Nothing can be hidden.

🚨

Suspicious Activity Alerts

The system automatically flags unusual behaviour — logins from new locations, repeated failures, token reuse — for admin review.

⚡

Instant Access Revocation

An Admin can instantly invalidate all active sessions for any user — terminating access across every device within seconds.

🔒 Security philosophy: Zero-Trust by design. Every request is verified independently — a valid username and password alone are not enough to submit attendance. The device must be registered, approved, and cryptographically authenticated.
